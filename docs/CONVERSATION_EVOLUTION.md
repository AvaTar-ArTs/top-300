# Evolution of TOP-300

## Stage 1 — Replicating an AI-video trend workflow

The initial investigation concerned whether a viral YouTube workflow for generating long AI videos was real and reproducible. The durable insight was orchestration rather than one generator.

## Stage 2 — Choosing topics with market demand

The focus moved upstream: identify what audiences are pulling toward and use trend signals as production input.

## Stage 3 — HOT-300

The first aggressive formulation targeted top 1–5% rising topics and +300% acceleration, while introducing supply-gap thinking.

## Stage 4 — Removing personal history

Personal-history weighting was explicitly removed. The forecasting engine became market-signal only.

## Stage 5 — Proper forecasting

+300% was recognized as detection, not forecasting. Targets became 24h/72h/7d breakout probabilities, persistence, lead-lag, propagation, peak/half-life, and demand-vs-supply opportunity.

## Stage 6 — Research scaffold repository

`AvaTar-ArTs/top-300` was created with methodology docs, a transparent feature baseline and tests.

## Stage 7 — End-to-end product

v1 converts the research into a runnable local-first product with immutable replay, feature extraction, learned/heuristic forecasting, ranking, evaluation and CLI delivery.
